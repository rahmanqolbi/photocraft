PhotoSoft app icon (photocraft.svg, photocraft-small.svg and every file derived
from them in this directory: photocraft-1024.png, photocraft.icns, photocraft.ico,
hicolor/).

An original geometric vector construction made for this project: a hand-written
parametric SVG (two caps built from a stem and semicircular annuli, a diagonal
capsule, a diamond accent). No third-party material and no traced artwork.
